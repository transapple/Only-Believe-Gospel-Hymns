ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

To God be the glory. With a humble and grateful heart, I thank all who made Only Believe Gospel Hymns possible, and I offer this work first of all to the Lord.

First, I thank God Almighty. He gave me the vision, the strength, and the patience to carry this work from a simple idea to a hymn book you can hold in your hand. Every step was by His grace, and every blessing this app brings belongs to Him.

I thank the Lord for the message of the hour brought by His servant, Brother William Marrion Branham. The reverence and depth of the hymns of this message planted the desire in me to preserve these songs and share them with the Bride of Christ everywhere.

To my beloved father, Elijah Alfred: thank you for your prayers, your counsel, and your faith in this work. When I grew tired, your encouragement kept me going. May the Lord reward you richly.

To my local church family: thank you for your prayers, your kind words, and your honest feedback. Your love for worship reminded me why this work matters.

To the elders and ministers who keep the Word alive in our assemblies: thank you for teaching us that singing is not entertainment but ministry, and for guarding the spirit in which these hymns were born.

To the unknown believers who first wrote, gathered, and sang these hymns: your names may be hidden from us, but your songs still carry the faith you held. Every verse here is your legacy.

To every believer and app user, young and old: thank you for downloading, singing, sharing, and sending suggestions. Because of you, the hymns now travel to places where a printed book may never reach, and they can be sung even offline.

I also thank the open-source community, the tutorial makers, and every free resource that taught me to build this app. I have never had formal training, but the generosity of others made it possible to keep learning.

To Brother Joshua from Eagle Revelation Tabernacle, Biiso: thank you for working so hard to type the entire Alur version of the hymns from paper into digital format. May the Lord reward you richly.

My gratitude also goes to Brother Mbabazi from Lifeboat Ministry, Kampala, for his support. He shared the Luganda version of the hymns, and he also contributed financially to the development of this app. May the Lord bless you abundantly.

Special thanks to Brother George Parombo and Brother Fred, among the first users of this app, who have used Only Believe Gospel Hymns faithfully since the beginning in April 2025. May the Lord bless you both.

Lastly, I want to acknowledge myself, Okello David, not out of pride, but with a humble heart. I undertook every part of this work—from compiling songs to designing and managing updates. And while I am still learning and growing, I count it a privilege to serve in this way.

May this app bless every soul who opens it, and may it glorify God now and forever.

Thank you all.

Only believe. Amen.

Lord Jesus Christ
William Branham
Elijah Alfred

User Guide
How to get the most out of the app
Getting Started
Only Believe Gospel Hymns brings you gospel hymns to read anytime, plus Believers Connect — a spiritual-only space to share testimonies, encourage other believers and chat privately. Everything is free to use.
Browsing & Reading Hymns
Open the Hymns tab and scroll or search to find a hymn. Tap a hymn to open it in the reader. Inside the reader you can increase or decrease the text size and switch reading themes from Settings so it's comfortable on your eyes, day or night.
Favorites & My Notebook
Tap the heart on any hymn to save it to Favorites so you can find it quickly later. Use My Notebook (under More) to jot down your own personal notes, prayers or reflections and keep them organized with titles, colors and tags.
Testimony Wall & Believers Connect
Open Believers Connect to create a simple believer profile with a photo from your device. From there you can find other believers, follow them, chat privately, and share a testimony on the Testimony Wall. You can like and comment on testimonies, and add a photo or Scripture reference to your own posts. Keep every post respectful and spiritual — that's what this community is for.
Editing & Deleting
Press and hold one of your own private chat messages to Edit or Delete it. Press and hold one of your own testimonies on the Testimony Wall to Edit or Delete it. Comments work the same way — press and hold your own comment. Every delete asks you to confirm first, since it can't be undone.
Account Settings
Inside Believers Connect, open the Find Believers tab and tap the ⚙ icon next to the search bar to reach Account Settings. From there you can edit your profile, change your password, log out, or delete your believer account. To use your account on another device, open Find Believers and log in with your username and password. If you forget your password, tap Forgot password and confirm your full name, username, phone number and pastor's name.
Recent Activity
My Recent Activities (under More) keeps a log of your last 30 actions — viewing a hymn, favoriting, adding a note, visiting Testimony Wall or Find Believers, and more. Tap any entry to jump straight back to it. Once you pass 30, the oldest entry drops off automatically.
Settings
In Settings you can choose your default hymn language, pick a reading theme, and adjust the default reading font size for every hymn you open.
Need more help?
If you're stuck or have a question, open Contact Us (under More) to call our support line directly on MTN or Airtel.

User Guide
How to get the most out of the app
Getting Started
Only Believe Gospel Hymns brings you gospel hymns to read anytime, plus Believers Connect — a spiritual-only space to share testimonies, encourage other believers and chat privately. Everything is free to use.
Browsing & Reading Hymns
Open the Hymns tab and scroll or search to find a hymn. Tap a hymn to open it in the reader. Inside the reader you can increase or decrease the text size and switch reading themes from Settings so it's comfortable on your eyes, day or night.
Favorites & My Notebook
Tap the heart on any hymn to save it to Favorites so you can find it quickly later. Use My Notebook (under More) to jot down your own personal notes, prayers or reflections and keep them organized with titles, colors and tags.
Testimony Wall & Believers Connect
Open Believers Connect to create a simple believer profile with a photo from your device. From there you can find other believers, follow them, chat privately, and share a testimony on the Testimony Wall. You can like and comment on testimonies, and add a photo or Scripture reference to your own posts. Keep every post respectful and spiritual — that's what this community is for.
Editing & Deleting
Press and hold one of your own private chat messages to Edit or Delete it. Press and hold one of your own testimonies on the Testimony Wall to Edit or Delete it. Comments work the same way — press and hold your own comment. Every delete asks you to confirm first, since it can't be undone.
Account Settings
Inside Believers Connect, open the Find Believers tab and tap the ⚙ icon next to the search bar to reach Account Settings. From there you can edit your profile, change your password, log out, or delete your believer account. To use your account on another device, open Find Believers and log in with your username and password. If you forget your password, tap Forgot password and confirm your full name, username, phone number and pastor's name.
Recent Activity
My Recent Activities (under More) keeps a log of your last 30 actions — viewing a hymn, favoriting, adding a note, visiting Testimony Wall or Find Believers, and more. Tap any entry to jump straight back to it. Once you pass 30, the oldest entry drops off automatically.
Settings
In Settings you can choose your default hymn language, pick a reading theme, and adjust the default reading font size for every hymn you open.
Need more help?
If you're stuck or have a question, open Contact Us (under More) to call our support line directly on MTN or Airtel.
